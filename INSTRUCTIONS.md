EZ-VENDO SYSTEM
How to Use

STEP 1: CONNECT TO WIFI
• Open WiFi settings on your phone or computer
• Find and connect to: EZ-Vendo-WIFI
• Wait until you see "Connected"

STEP 2: OPEN BROWSER
• Open any web browser
• Type: 192.168.1.1
• Press Enter or Go

STEP 3: SCAN YOUR CARD
• Place your RFID card on the scanner
• Wait for beep sound
• Check the screen for your status

STEP 4: GET INTERNET ACCESS
• Green light + 3 beeps = Access granted
• Red light + long beep = Access denied
• You can now use the internet

TROUBLESHOOTING

WiFi won't connect?
→ Make sure you're near the device
→ Turn WiFi off and on again

Can't open 192.168.1.1?
→ Check you're on EZ-Vendo-WIFI network
→ Try: http://192.168.1.1

Card not working?
→ Place card flat on scanner
→ Wait 2 seconds between scans
→ Try again
